## Method

Evidence comes from public pages fetched on the date shown: home page, product page, one case study, one industry page, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemaps (for URL counts), plus G2/Capterra and Wikipedia/Wikidata. For large companies (Schaeffler, SKF, Fluke, Banner, Acoem), only the condition-monitoring section was assessed. Anything that could not be confirmed is marked "not verified" in the profile.

Not covered yet: rankings and traffic (no SEO tool), backlink profiles, and what AI assistants actually answer (that is the monthly check in `05-tracking/ai-visibility/`).

## Re-checking

Quarterly, or when a competitor launches something. Update the profile's `Checked` date, note what changed at the top of the profile, then update the matrix. Adding a competitor: copy `_template.md`, add a row above.
