## How to review

For each case study there's a new **title** (shown as the page heading, on the case-study cards and in Google) and a new **meta description** (the two-line text under the title in Google), in English and Polish. Mark anything to change; approving with "OK" is enough.

**What stays the same:** page addresses (URLs), page content, images. Only titles and descriptions change, so no links break.

**Rules we followed:**
- **No client names** in titles or descriptions (Viezo, 2026-10-21). Today 25 of 30 descriptions name the client ("…at Ulmas", "…at Volkswagen Polska"); none will after this. The names stay inside the case-study pages as they are now.
- **Machine brand names stay** where they describe the equipment (IMA, Becker, Hymmen, Bosch Rexroth). People search for them ("ima bearing": 51 Google impressions), and they name a machine, not a client.
- Each title says **what failed, on which machine, and the result**, in words people search for. Search Console already shows impressions for "exhaust fan bearing" (181), "conveyor gearbox repair" (168), "fan fault" (96), "gearbox fault detected" (85) and "motor defect" (50), but almost no clicks, because today's titles ("FAN FAULT", "MOTOR DEFECT") don't say anything beyond that.
- Sentence case in the data. The site still displays headings in capitals (that's the design), but Google shows the title as written.
- No numbers beyond what the page already states. No new claims: everything comes from the page itself.
- Google shows about 60 characters of a title. In Google, each title gets a suffix: " | Viezo case study" (EN) and " | Studium przypadku Viezo" (PL), set once in Yoast.

## Open points for Viezo

1. **Industry wording:** we describe each client by industry only ("furniture plant", "automotive plant", "chocolate factory"). Is any of these too revealing? For example, "chocolate factory" or "automotive plant" in Poland could point to a specific company.
2. **One URL contains a client name:** `kekava-ammonia-compressor-fault`. (IMA in other URLs is the machine brand.) Changing a URL needs a redirect and briefly resets Google's history for that page, so we suggest leaving it unless the client objects.
3. **Polish wording:** written by us and should be checked by a Polish speaker, especially technical terms (agregat, frez, piła podcinająca).
