Viezo SEO & GEO Plan

Findings from a technical and content audit of viezo.lt, and a plan to grow visibility in Google and in AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot), split into what can be done this week and what follows over the next six months.

Scope: all 94 URLs in the site's XML sitemaps (EN + PL) Checked: crawlability, indexing signals, metadata, headings, structured data, page weight, server setup
Summary

What matters most

The site has a solid base: clean URLs, Yoast sitemaps, working canonicals, EN/PL hreflang, fast server responses (median 471 ms) and genuinely strong case studies with named clients and measured results. The main problems are technical blockers that are cheap to fix, and content that is too thin and too unstructured to win searches or be quoted by AI assistants.

All images Blocked from Google

robots.txt disallows /wp-content/uploads/, so Google cannot crawl any uploaded image: no image search, weaker page understanding, broken previews in some results.

~70 MB Video on the home page

A 49 MB hero video and a 21 MB video load on the home page. On mobile this ruins load speed (Core Web Vitals) and costs visitors' data.

0 gzip No compression, HTTP/1.1

HTML, CSS and JS are sent uncompressed (home HTML 137 KB, CSS 135 KB instead of roughly 25 KB each). The server runs HTTP/1.1, OpenSSL 1.0.2 and Amazon Linux 2 (end of life June 2026).

No entity data Search engines and AI don't know who Viezo is

No Organization, Product or Article structured data, no llms.txt, and the brand domain viezo.com is a parked page. AI assistants have little to anchor "Viezo" and "Sonora" to.

59 case studies Great material, under-used

Median 152 words, generic all-caps titles like "FAN BEARING WEAR – Viezo". They are the site's most citable asset and should be its main traffic driver.

GA4 live Measurement restarted 12 Oct

The old Universal Analytics tag had been dead since 2023. GA4 is now installed with consent mode; Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools still need to be connected.

Findings

Detailed findings

Each finding lists the evidence from the crawl, why it matters, and the fix. Severity: High blocks rankings or costs a lot of traffic, Medium limits growth, Low is polish.

Technical

robots.txt blocks all uploaded images

High
Evidence

Disallow: /wp-content/uploads/. Every case study photo, logo and product image sits in that folder (926 images across the crawled pages).

Why

Google can't index or understand the images, case studies can't appear in image results, and Google renders pages without their visuals.

Fix

Remove that line from robots.txt (in Yoast → Tools → File editor, or the file itself). Then request re-crawl in Search Console.

Home page loads about 70 MB of video

High
Evidence

Indutrial-Web-video-main-page-1-1.mp4 is 49 MB, Sonora-Alert-System-Overview_21mb.mp4 is 21 MB. Case-study thumbnails are PNGs of about 300 KB at 460×380 px; none of the 294 case-study images are WebP.

Why

Largest Contentful Paint and mobile experience are ranking factors. On a phone connection the hero alone takes tens of seconds and many visitors leave before it plays.

Fix

Re-encode the hero to about 3–5 MB (720p, H.264 + WebM, 10–15 s loop), show a poster image first, and load the second video only when it is played. Convert thumbnails to WebP (about 30–50 KB each).

No compression, old server stack

High
Evidence

No Content-Encoding on HTML, CSS or JS. HTTP/1.1 only. Apache/2.4.62 OpenSSL/1.0.2k on Amazon Linux 2 (EOL June 2026, 146 pending security updates).

Why

Every page ships 3–5× more bytes than needed. HTTP/1.1 loads files one by one. The OS no longer receives security fixes.

Fix

Now: enable gzip (mod_deflate) via .htaccess. Later: move to Amazon Linux 2023 or a managed host with HTTP/2, Brotli and current PHP, or put the site behind Cloudflare (free plan covers compression, HTTP/2/3 and caching).

viezo.com is a parked domain

Medium
Evidence

https://viezo.com returns a blank page that redirects to /lander (a domain-parking page).

Why

People and AI assistants guessing "viezo.com" land on a parking page, and the brand signal is split.

Fix

Confirm who owns it. If Viezo owns it, 301-redirect it to viezo.lt. If not, consider buying it; for an international B2B brand, .com is worth having.

Thin or empty pages in the index

Medium
Evidence

Terms of Use has 3 words (EN) and 4 words (PL). PL EU funds page has 22 words. 15 case-study category pages have 32–240 words and no meta description. 4 near-identical "EU Funding" posts.

Why

Empty and near-duplicate pages dilute the site's quality signals and waste crawl attention.

Fix

Fill in the Terms page or set it to noindex. Turn the category pages into industry landing pages (see plan) or noindex them until then. Merge the EU funding posts into the EU funds page.

hreflang has no x-default

Low
Evidence

Pages declare hreflang="en" and "pl" but no x-default. One EN case study (granulator bearing fault) has no language links at all.

Fix

Add x-default pointing to English (Polylang setting), and link the missing translation.

On-page content

Titles and headings don't say what the page is about

High
Evidence
  • 36 of 59 case-study titles are all caps without context, for example "FAN BEARING WEAR – Viezo".
  • H1 of the Sonora product page is just "Sonora"; Pricing is "Pricing"; Case studies is "CASE STUDIES".
  • Both Contact pages (EN and PL) have no H1 at all.
Why

Title and H1 are the strongest on-page signals. "Sonora" alone matches nobody's search; "wireless vibration monitoring system" does.

Fix

Rewrite titles and H1s around what buyers search for (see examples below). For case studies, use a fixed pattern with the fault, the machine, the client and "case study".

Missing or wrong meta descriptions

Medium
Evidence

27 of 94 URLs have none: Contact (EN, PL), Privacy, Terms, EU pages, the 4 EU posts and 15 category pages. The Polish case-study archive uses the English description.

Fix

Write descriptions for every indexable page (about 140–160 characters, with a reason to click). Fix the Polish archive.

Case studies are short and not quotable

Medium
Evidence

Median 152 words; 58 of 59 are under 250 words. The structure is good (client, industry, equipment, issue, results) but there are no dates in the title, no numbers in a summary, no charts, quotes or FAQ.

Why

Google ranks pages that fully answer a query, and AI assistants quote pages with clear, specific facts. "Volkswagen Polska cut belt repair from 1 hour to 15 minutes" is exactly what an AI answer cites, if it can find it in a clear block.

Fix

A case-study template with a "Key facts" box at the top (client, industry, machine, fault, detected how early, outcome in numbers), a vibration chart, a short client quote and 2–3 FAQs. Aim for 400–600 words.

Images without alt text

Low
Evidence

100 of 926 images have no alt text: 10 on the home page, 17–18 on the Sonora pages, 12 on case studies.

Fix

Add descriptive alt texts in the Media Library (machine, fault, sensor). Helps accessibility and image search once robots.txt is fixed.

Structured data and GEO (AI search)

No structured data about the company, product or case studies

High
Evidence

Only Yoast's generic WebSite, WebPage and BreadcrumbList. No Organization (logo, address, LinkedIn), no Product for Sonora, no Article on case studies, no FAQPage.

Why

Structured data is how Google's Knowledge Graph and AI systems connect "Viezo", "Sonora", the address in Vilnius and the LinkedIn page into one entity. Without it, brand searches and AI answers are less reliable.

Fix

Add Organization (with sameAs to LinkedIn and other profiles), Product/SoftwareApplication for Sonora, Article on every case study (with client and industry as about/mentions), and FAQPage where FAQs exist. Can be done in the theme together with Yoast.

Nothing written for AI assistants

Medium
Evidence

No llms.txt. No pages answer the questions buyers ask an assistant, such as "what is predictive maintenance", "how does wireless vibration monitoring work", "how much does it cost", "how many sensors per machine". The blog has 4 posts, all about EU funding. AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) are allowed by robots.txt, which is good.

Why

AI answers are assembled from pages that state facts plainly, define terms, give numbers and are backed by other sources. Viezo has the expertise but no pages that express it in that form.

Fix

Publish an llms.txt (company, product, key pages, case studies) and a small set of expert explainer pages in EN and PL (see plan). Write each with a direct 2–3 sentence answer at the top, then detail, data and a link to relevant case studies.

Weak presence outside the site

Medium
Evidence

The site links only to LinkedIn. No sign of review-platform listings, a Google Business Profile or a Wikidata entry.

Why

Both Google and AI assistants trust brands that are mentioned consistently in other places. Clients like Volkswagen Polska, IMA Schelling and furniture manufacturers are strong references if they are visible beyond viezo.lt.

Fix

Create or complete Google Business Profile, G2 and Capterra listings, Crunchbase and a Wikidata item; list Viezo on partners' and distributors' sites (Techvitas, Ruven, Sujan, TGD, IMA Schelling) with a link; pitch 2–3 case studies to industry media.

Measurement

No search data yet

Medium
Evidence

Analytics collected nothing between July 2023 and 12 October 2026 (dead Universal Analytics tag). GA4 now runs with consent mode. Search Console access was not available for this audit.

Fix

Connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools (Bing powers ChatGPT search and Copilot), submit the sitemap in both, link Search Console to GA4, and add a GA4 report for AI referrals (chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, copilot, gemini).

Limits of this audit: no access to Search Console, so actual rankings, queries and indexing status were not checked. Google's PageSpeed service was unavailable on the day, so speed findings come from measured file sizes and server headers, not Lighthouse scores. No backlink tool was used. All checks were read-only requests to public pages.
